Supplier Payments — Payment Advice

Pay many invoices of one supplier with a single approved document, by hand or on a schedule.

App: sf_trading
Module: Sf Trading
Updated: 26 July 2026

What this does

A Payment Advice authorises payment against several outstanding documents of one supplier, in one document, with one approval. ERPNext's own Payment Request cannot do this — a Payment Request covers a single document, so "pay these nine invoices for this supplier" had nowhere to live.

It is not limited to Purchase Invoices. An advice can reference Purchase Invoices, Sales Invoices, Journal Entries, Expense Claims, Purchase Orders and Sales Orders. In practice a supplier's outstanding list often includes Journal Entries as well as invoices, and those come through automatically.

Outstanding figures are not calculated by this app. They come from the same ERPNext engine the Payment Entry form uses, so part-payments, credit notes and return invoices are already netted off before you see them.

Raise one advice

Use this when you are paying a single supplier.

1
New Payment Advice. Set Company, Party Type (Supplier) and the Party. Every other picker — bank account, cost centre, project — is filtered to that company automatically.
2
Get Outstanding Documents. The dialog offers the same filters as the Payment Entry form: posting-date range, due-date range, outstanding amount window, cost centre, and whether to include orders to be billed. Rows already on the advice are never added twice.
3
Set Payment Amount. This is what you are authorising. It is spread across the references from the oldest down. A row beyond the authorised amount stays on the advice with zero allocated, so you can still see the full picture. The headline above the table shows, for example, "2,464.713 of 3,000.000 allocated across 18 of 21 references".
4
Submit. If an approval workflow is switched on, use the workflow actions instead (see Approval). Otherwise only the user linked to the selected Approver may submit.
An invoice can only sit on one live advice. If it is already on another advice — even a draft — you will be told which one, so nothing gets paid twice.

Batch by supplier — the Builder

3
Tick what you are paying. Tick a whole supplier, or individual invoices inside it. The footer keeps a running count and total.
4
Advice Options (optional) — mode of payment, company bank account, approver, cost centre, remarks, and whether to submit. Set once, applied to every advice in the batch.
5
Create Advices. You confirm a count and a total first. Each advice is created as a Draft unless you chose to submit. The result panel links every advice, and lists anything skipped with its reason. Batches over 15 suppliers run in the background and chime when finished.

From the Purchase Invoice list

Tick invoices in the Purchase Invoice list → Actions → Create Payment Advice (by supplier). Invoices are grouped by supplier and one draft advice is raised per supplier. Anything already on a live advice is skipped and named, not silently dropped.

All ticked invoices must belong to one company. Invoices with nothing outstanding are ignored.

Creating the Payment Entry

On a submitted advice press Create Payment Entry. The advice's allocations become the Payment Entry's references, and ERPNext completes the rest (base amounts, party balance, unallocated amount).

EventWhat happens to the advice
Payment Entry submittedAdvice is stamped with the Payment Entry and date; status becomes Paid or Partly Paid; every reference row's status and outstanding refresh
Payment Entry cancelledStamp is cleared, advice returns to Approved, rows refresh — a corrected Payment Entry can then be raised
Advice cancelledBlocked while its Payment Entry is still submitted. Cancel the Payment Entry first
Two submitted Payment Entries can never claim the same advice, and each invoice shows its advice under Connections on the invoice itself.

Scheduled runs

Payment Automation Settings — one configuration per company and party type.

A run does as much as you allow, and each step needs the one before it:

1 · Create Payment Advice 2 · Submit Payment Advice 3 · Create Payment Entry 4 · Submit Payment Entry

The form spells the plan out in words, for example: "Will create advices → submit advices on Mon, Tue at 07:00". Set the weekdays and the time; the run fires on the first scheduler tick at or after that time and only once a day.

✓
Dry Run works out exactly what would happen and reports it without creating anything. Use it first, always.
▶
Run Now ignores the schedule and runs immediately.
Start with step 1 only and Dry Run ticked. Read the summary, then enable more. Step 4 moves money.

Each run reports through the chime, the desk bell, and email where an outgoing email account exists. Quiet runs stay quiet unless you ask to be told about them.

Payment Entry thresholdAbove this, Payment Entries are left as drafts
Exclude foreign currencySkip suppliers with invoices outside the company currency

A single supplier can be excluded from every run by ticking Disable Automatic Payment on the Supplier — no configuration change needed.

Troubleshooting

SymptomCause & fix
Bank, IBAN and SWIFT are blank on the adviceThe supplier has no Bank Account record. Create one and the details fetch automatically
"Payment Amount exceeds the total payable"You authorised more than the references add up to. Fetch more invoices or lower the amount
The invoice picker is emptySet the Company first — the picker is filtered to it, and to submitted invoices only
"Cancel Payment Entry … before cancelling this advice"Cancel the Payment Entry, then the advice
No chime when a run finishesClick anywhere on the page once per session — browsers block sound until you interact
No email summaryNo outgoing Email Account is configured. Chime and bell still work; email starts once one exists
The scheduled run never firesCheck Enabled, the weekday ticks, the time, and that at least step 1 is on
